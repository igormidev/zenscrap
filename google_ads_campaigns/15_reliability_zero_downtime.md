## Performance Tips

1. **Front-load benefits** - Start descriptions with the most compelling benefit in first 5-7 words for mobile visibility
2. **Use specific numbers** - "24/7 monitoring" outperforms vague reliability claims
3. **Address pain directly** - Match problem keywords with problem-focused headlines
4. **Avoid over-pinning** - Pin maximum 2 headlines to allow Google's ML optimization
5. **Test emotional angles** - "Never miss data again" vs "Zero downtime" to find what resonates
6. **Include trust signals** - "Try it free" reduces friction and improves CTR

## Google Ads Best Practices Applied

This campaign follows 2025-2026 Google Search Ads best practices:

1. **Diverse headlines** - 6 unique headlines covering different angles (reliability, monitoring, benefits, pain points)
2. **Benefit-first copy** - Descriptions lead with outcomes users care about, not feature lists
3. **Emotional triggers** - "Never miss data again" and "Stop waking up to broken scrapers" create urgency
4. **Specific claims** - "24/7 monitoring" is more compelling than generic "always reliable"
5. **Clear CTAs** - "Try it free" in descriptions prompts immediate action
6. **Problem/Solution balance** - Mix of problem-aware and solution-focused headlines
7. **Character optimization** - All copy stays well within limits while maximizing impact
