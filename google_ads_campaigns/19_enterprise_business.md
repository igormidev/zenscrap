## Performance Tips

1. **Front-load benefits** - Put the most compelling value in the first 5-7 words of descriptions since mobile truncates
2. **Use business language** - Words like "enterprise," "professional," "team," and "pipeline" resonate with B2B buyers
3. **Highlight visibility** - Business users want control; emphasize tracking, monitoring, and analytics
4. **Avoid technical jargon** - Focus on outcomes (data pipelines) not methods (CSS selectors)
5. **Lead with outcomes** - B2B buyers care about solving problems, not product features
6. **Include social proof** - If possible, add sitelink extensions with case studies or testimonials
7. **Match buying stage** - Enterprise keywords may indicate research phase; provide educational content

## Campaign Settings Recommendations

- **Network:** Google Search only (no Display - B2B search intent is high value)
- **Bidding Strategy:** Start with Maximize Conversions, transition to Target CPA after 50+ conversions (B2B requires quality over quantity)
- **Location:** United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Germany, Netherlands (enterprise markets)
- **Device:** All devices initially; B2B often converts on desktop, monitor and adjust bids
- **Ad Schedule:** Business hours weighted higher (adjust based on conversion data)
- **Audience Segments:** Layer in-market audiences for "Business Services" and "Software" to refine targeting
