# Google Ads Campaign: Free Trial / No Signup Focus

**Campaign Theme:** Zero friction to try - no credit card required, no signup needed to test, see results before committing.

**Target Network:** Google Search only (google.com)

**Product:** ZenScrap - AI-powered web scraping platform where users describe what they want to extract in plain language. The AI builds, tests, and maintains scrapers automatically. Users can chat with AI, build their scraper, and test it live before ever creating an account.

## Campaign Settings Recommendations

### Bidding Strategy
- Start with **Maximize Clicks** to gather data
- Move to **Target CPA** or **Maximize Conversions** after 30+ conversions

### Audience Targeting
- **In-market audiences:** Software development, Business automation, Data analytics
- **Affinity audiences:** Developers, Tech enthusiasts, Business professionals

### Geographic Targeting
- United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia (English-speaking, high LTV)
- Expand to Germany, France, Netherlands based on performance

### Device Targeting
- Desktop: Primary (developers more likely on desktop)
- Mobile: Secondary (adjust bids -20% initially, optimize based on data)

### Ad Schedule
- Business hours: Mon-Fri 8am-8pm (increase bids +15%)
- Weekends: Maintain presence (developers often work weekends)

## Landing Page Recommendations

The landing page should:
1. **Hero section** with URL input field visible immediately
2. **Trust badges** prominently showing "No credit card" and "No signup"
3. **Live demo** or video showing the process
4. **Clear CTA** matching ad copy: "Try Free - No Signup"
5. **Social proof** (if available): user count, testimonials

---

## Performance Benchmarks

Based on industry data for SaaS free trial campaigns:
- **Target CTR:** 5-8% (free trial messaging typically performs above average)
- **Target CPC:** Monitor and optimize based on conversion value
- **Target Conversion Rate:** 15-25% for free trial signups (no card barrier)

---

## A/B Testing Plan

### Week 1-2: Headline Testing
- Test "No Signup" vs "No Credit Card" as primary messaging
- Test "Free Trial" vs "Try Free" vs "Test Free"

### Week 3-4: Description Testing
- Test benefit-led (AI builds your scraper) vs friction-removal-led (no signup needed)
- Test specific time claims (2 minutes) vs general speed claims (instantly)

### Week 5+: Optimization
- Pause underperforming headlines/descriptions
- Double down on winning combinations
- Test new variations of winners

---

## Notes

- **Key differentiator:** Users can build AND test their scraper before any signup - this is rare in the market
- **Primary objection addressed:** "I don't want to give my credit card for something I haven't tried"
- **Secondary objection addressed:** "I don't want to create another account"
- The campaign targets users who are actively looking for low-friction ways to try web scraping tools
