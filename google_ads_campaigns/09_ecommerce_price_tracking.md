## Campaign Settings Recommendations

- **Network:** Google Search only
- **Bid Strategy:** Maximize clicks initially, then optimize for conversions
- **Ad Schedule:** Business hours (when pricing analysts are working)
- **Device Targeting:** Desktop primary (business users), mobile secondary
- **Location:** US, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, Netherlands (major e-commerce markets)

## Seasonal Considerations

**High-Volume Periods:**
- Q4 (Oct-Dec): Holiday pricing competition peaks
- Black Friday / Cyber Monday prep (September-November)
- Prime Day season (July)
- Back-to-school (August)

**Recommended Budget Increases:**
- Increase budget 30-50% during peak e-commerce seasons
- Focus on urgency messaging during competitive pricing periods
