## Key Best Practices Applied

1. **Unique value proposition** - Emphasizes the conversational AI interface that differentiates ZenScrap from traditional scrapers
2. **Benefit-focused headlines** - "Just Chat, AI Builds It" focuses on outcome, not features
3. **Relatable comparison** - "Like ChatGPT for web scraping" leverages familiar AI assistant concepts
4. **Action-oriented CTAs** - "Try free", "Get Your Data" encourage immediate action
5. **Character optimization** - All assets use available space efficiently without exceeding limits
6. **Multiple headline angles** - Problem-solving ("Talk to AI, Get Your Data"), solution-focused ("AI Scraping Assistant"), experience-focused ("Conversational Web Scraping")
7. **Trust signals** - "No signup to test", "Ready in under 2 minutes", "24/7 AI assistant"
8. **Front-loaded benefits** - Descriptions start with the key benefit (chat, describe, AI) to remain visible on mobile truncation
9. **Emotional appeal** - "Like having an expert to talk to" creates connection and reduces complexity anxiety
10. **Varied headline lengths** - Mix of 21-27 characters provides flexibility for different ad placements
