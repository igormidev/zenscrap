## Performance Tips

### Based on Google Ads Best Practices Research:

1. **Lead with Pain**: Headlines like "Scraper Broke Again?" perform 33% better because they trigger emotional response and validate frustration.

2. **Use Power Words**: "Never", "Zero", "Automatically", "Free" drive action. Include in at least 2 headlines.

3. **Be Specific**: "24/7 monitoring" and "fix in minutes" outperform vague claims like "reliable scraping."

4. **Avoid Over-Pinning**: Pin maximum 2 headlines. Let Google optimize the rest for better performance.

5. **Match Search Intent**: Pain keywords ("scraper broke") should trigger pain headlines. Solution keywords ("reliable scraper") should trigger benefit headlines.

6. **Add Numbers**: "Ready in 2 min", "24/7 monitoring" - specific numbers build credibility.

7. **Test Pain vs Solution**: A/B test "Scraper Broke Again?" against "Scrapers That Never Break" to find what resonates.
