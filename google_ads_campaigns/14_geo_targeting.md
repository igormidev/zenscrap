## Performance Tips

1. **Highlight the automation angle** - Users searching geo-targeting keywords are tired of managing proxies manually
2. **Use specific numbers** - "195+ countries" is more compelling than "worldwide"
3. **Address the pain point** - Focus on eliminating proxy management overhead
4. **Include use cases** - Localized prices and regional data resonate with commercial intent
5. **Emphasize AI automation** - The fact that AI selects locations automatically is a key differentiator
6. **Test regional language** - Some users search "international," others search "global" or "worldwide"

## Campaign Settings Recommendations

- **Network:** Google Search only (no Display)
- **Bidding Strategy:** Start with Maximize Clicks, transition to Target CPA after 30+ conversions
- **Location:** United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Germany, France (English and tech-savvy markets with high intent)
- **Device:** All devices (monitor mobile vs desktop performance)
- **Ad Schedule:** All hours initially, optimize based on conversion data
- **Budget Tip:** Higher CPCs expected for competitive geo-proxy keywords - start with sufficient daily budget to gather data
