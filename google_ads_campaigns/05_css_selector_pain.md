# Google Ads Campaign: CSS Selector Pain Point Focus

## Campaign Theme
Target developers and data professionals frustrated with the tedious, fragile nature of CSS selectors and XPath expressions in web scraping. Focus on the pain of hunting through HTML, dealing with selectors that break when sites update, and the relief of simply describing what you want in plain language.

## Target Audience Notes

**Primary Audience:**
- Developers who have experienced the frustration of maintaining CSS selectors
- Data analysts who need web data but lack deep technical skills
- Teams tired of scrapers breaking after every site update

**Search Intent:**
- Users actively searching for alternatives to manual selector writing
- Frustrated developers looking for "web scraping without css" solutions
- People who have just had their scraper break due to site changes

**Emotional Triggers Used:**
- Relief from tedious work (escaping CSS hell)
- Fear of missing out on easier solutions
- Frustration acknowledgment (validates their pain)
- Curiosity about AI-powered alternatives

## Campaign Settings Recommendations

- **Network:** Google Search only
- **Bid Strategy:** Maximize clicks initially, then optimize for conversions
- **Ad Schedule:** Weekdays, business hours (when developers are working)
- **Device Targeting:** Desktop primary (developers), mobile secondary
- **Location:** US, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany (English-speaking dev markets)
