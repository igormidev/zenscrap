**Unique Selling Proposition:** Unlike competitors that only provide scraping APIs or require manual configuration, ZenScrap's AI builds, tests, and maintains scrapers automatically from plain language descriptions. Self-healing technology eliminates the maintenance burden that plagues traditional scraping tools.

## Ad Copy Best Practices Applied

Based on 2025-2026 Google Ads research:

1. **Highlight Clear USPs** - Every headline emphasizes what competitors lack (AI creation, self-healing, no code)

2. **Address Searcher Intent** - People searching "[competitor] alternative" are actively comparing; speak directly to their pain

3. **Use Specific Claims** - "Plain language" and "no CSS selectors" are concrete, not vague like "better" or "easier"

4. **Include Social Proof Signals** - "Try free" reduces risk; competitors require setup investment

5. **Avoid Trademark Issues** - We use competitor names in keywords only, not in display URLs (legal gray area avoided)

6. **Match Landing Page** - Each ad group should point to a dedicated comparison landing page

## Campaign Settings Recommendations

- **Network:** Google Search only (disable Search Partners initially)
- **Bidding Strategy:** Target Impression Share (top of page) - competitor campaigns need visibility
- **Daily Budget:** Higher than average - competitor keywords have elevated CPCs
- **Location:** United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Germany, Netherlands
- **Device:** All devices (developers search on desktop and mobile)
- **Ad Schedule:** Business hours weighted higher (B2B decision-makers)

---

## Expected Performance Notes

Competitor campaigns typically show:
- **Higher CPC** than brand or generic keywords (expect 2-3x)
- **Lower CTR** than brand campaigns (3-5% vs 8-10%)
- **Higher conversion intent** - searchers are actively evaluating alternatives
- **Longer consideration cycle** - use remarketing to nurture

Monitor Quality Score closely. If below 5, revisit ad-to-landing-page alignment.

Test these variations after 1,000+ impressions per variant:
