## Performance Tips

1. **Include numbers when possible** - "Extract data in 2 minutes" performs better than vague claims
2. **Use specific data types** - Mentioning "prices, articles, profiles" is more compelling than generic "data"
3. **Emphasize output quality** - "Clean, structured data" addresses a major pain point
4. **A/B test angles** - Test problem-focused vs solution-focused copy
5. **Match search intent** - Users searching "extract prices from website" want specific solutions
6. **Monitor Quality Score** - Aim for 7+ by ensuring landing page matches ad messaging
7. **Use ad extensions** - Sitelinks and callouts increase CTR by 10-15% on average

## Key Best Practices Applied

Based on [Google Ads best practices research](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/google-ads-copy):

1. **Benefit over features** - Headlines focus on what users get (any data, any site) not technical specs
2. **Specific CTAs** - "Try free, no signup" removes friction and encourages action
3. **Action words** - "Extract", "Get", "Turn" are strong action verbs that drive clicks
4. **Emotional trigger** - "Any website, any complexity" reduces anxiety about difficult sites
5. **Social proof implied** - "Works on complex sites" suggests proven capability
6. **Trust signals** - "No signup", "Free trial", "Ready in minutes" reduce perceived risk
7. **Varied headline lengths** - Mix of 19-25 characters for different ad placements
8. **Multiple angles** - Problem-focused, solution-focused, and benefit-focused variations
