## Performance Tips

1. **Use Ad Strength indicator** - Aim for "Excellent" by providing diverse headlines
2. **Avoid over-pinning** - Pin maximum 2 headlines to allow Google's optimization
3. **A/B test descriptions** - Rotate between problem-focused and solution-focused copy
4. **Include numbers when possible** - "24/7 monitoring" performs better than generic claims
5. **Match search intent** - Use problem keywords with problem-focused headlines

- **Device:** All devices (monitor mobile vs desktop performance)
- **Ad Schedule:** All hours initially, optimize based on conversion data
