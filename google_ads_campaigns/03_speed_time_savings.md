## Campaign Settings Recommendations

### Bidding Strategy
- Start with: **Maximize Clicks** (for initial data)
- Transition to: **Target CPA** once you have 30+ conversions

### Geographic Targeting
- Primary: United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia
- Secondary: Germany, Netherlands, France (English speakers)

### Device Targeting
- Desktop: 100% bid adjustment (primary user base)
- Mobile: Start at 100%, adjust based on conversion data
- Tablet: 100%

### Ad Schedule
- Weekdays: Focus budget 9 AM - 6 PM (business hours)
- Weekends: Reduced but active (developers often work weekends)

### Audience Targeting (Observation Mode)
- In-market: Business Software, Web Development Tools
- Affinity: Technology Enthusiasts, Business Professionals
- Custom Intent: People searching for web scraping, API tools, data extraction
