---

## Performance Tips

1. **Highlight social proof** - "Community-tested" and usage stats build trust
2. **Use specific numbers** - "1000+ scrapers" outperforms vague claims
3. **Name popular sites** - Amazon, eBay, LinkedIn have high search intent
4. **Emphasize time savings** - "Start in minutes" vs building from scratch
5. **Include "free" where authentic** - "Try free" or "Test any scraper free" drives clicks
6. **Match search intent** - Site-specific keywords get site-specific headlines

## Campaign Settings Recommendations

- **Network:** Google Search only (no Display)
- **Bidding Strategy:** Start with Maximize Clicks, transition to Target CPA after 30+ conversions
- **Location:** United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Germany (high-intent markets)
- **Device:** All devices (monitor mobile vs desktop performance)
- **Ad Schedule:** All hours initially, optimize based on conversion data
- **Audience Signals:** Software developers, data analysts, business intelligence professionals
